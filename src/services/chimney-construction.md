## New chimney construction

Adding a masonry fireplace or a wood stove? We build new brick chimneys designed for the appliance they'll serve. That includes:

- A properly sized **flue** for good draft
- **Clearances to combustibles** as required by building code
- A **flue liner** suited to the fuel type
- A sloped **crown** with an overhang to shed water
- Correct **height above the roof** for draft and safety
- **Flashing** to tie the chimney into the roof without leaks
- A **chimney cap** with a spark arrestor

## Chimney rebuilds

Some chimneys can't be repaired economically. Common reasons for a **partial rebuild** (typically from the roofline up) or a **full rebuild**:

- Extensive spalling, crumbling brick and failed mortar
- A chimney that's leaning or separating from the house
- Serious earthquake damage
- Long-term water damage that has weakened the structure
- A crown and upper courses that have deteriorated beyond patching

We'll only recommend a rebuild when repair truly won't do the job, and we'll show you why.

## How a construction project works

1. **Assessment.** We inspect the existing chimney or the planned location and talk through what you want to use it for.
2. **Scope and quote.** You get a written scope of work and a clear price.
3. **Permits.** Chimney construction generally requires a building permit. We'll talk through permit requirements for your city before work begins.
4. **Build.** Site protection, then masonry work, liner, crown, flashing and cap.
5. **Final walkthrough.** We walk through the finished work with you and clean up the site.
